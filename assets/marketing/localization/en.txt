PIZZA CHIEF — Make It. Bake It.

Become the chef in a cozy pizza kitchen! Build 20 pizzas from 28 ingredients, control the oven heat, and turn your pizza at just the right moment.

Practice freely, finish three-order shifts, or take on the daily challenge. Earn XP, unlock recipes, collect up to three stars per pizza, and beat your personal best.

Make a Margherita in a guided lesson with no timer. Cook with 2–6 players in Pizza Battle, or send a friend a challenge code to beat your score. Play on computer, phone, or tablet, with portrait and landscape layouts on mobile.
